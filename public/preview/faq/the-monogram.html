    Is there an emergency dentist open now in Glasgow?

    Yes. Day Night Dental runs a 24-hour emergency helpline and keeps same-day appointments open every day. You can call any time, including evenings and weekends, so there’s no need to sit in pain until Monday.

    Day Night Dental, Merchant City

  • II
    DN II

    Are you taking on new patients?

    Yes, we’re welcoming new patients. The quickest way to start is to put your name down through our registration form, and we’ll be in touch to arrange your first visit. If we’re especially busy there can be a short wait for a routine appointment, but urgent and emergency care is always available, day or night.

    Day Night Dental, Merchant City

  • III
    DN III

    Where is the practice and where can I park?

    You’ll find us in Merchant City in central Glasgow, a short walk from Queen Street and Argyle Street stations. There are a few car parks nearby and on-street parking close to the door.

    Day Night Dental, Merchant City

  • IV
    DN IV

    What are your opening hours?

    We’re open in the practice Monday to Friday 7am to 11pm, Saturday 8am to 10pm and Sunday 9am to 9pm. Outside those hours our 24-hour emergency line is there for urgent dental problems.

    Day Night Dental, Merchant City
