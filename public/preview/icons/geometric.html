Icon system · dental family

The Constructed Bevel set.

Seven dental marks drawn by one hand on a single geometric grid: a shared 18px centre axis, one molar silhouette, 1px stroke, round caps and a consistent 3px radius.

Style in one line: geometric-precision line icons built from a common molar skeleton and a recurring two-stroke shine, so every mark reads as a member of the same designed system. All seven use currentColor only, so they inherit solid gold or solid blue with no gold-to-blue blend. Legible at 24px, refined at 72px.

The family, at 28 / 48 / 72px

Optically sized so the everyday tooth, the urgent cross, the aligner tray and the implant all carry the same weight on the line. Odd cells gold, even cells blue, to prove both brand colours.
