Seven marks drawn as one hand: the logo's single hairline, round caps and soft corner rhythm, lifted with one tinted accent shape for depth. Gold carries the daytime, planned care; blue carries the night and the urgent. One accent per icon, never a gold-into-blue blend. Everything is currentColor, so a chip's side decides the tint.
